Siegelings TCG — art-first home & navigation

A minimalist, gallery-led direction for the mobile hub: roughly 70% artwork, 20% navigation, 10% text. Every Siegeling below is real production overlay art pulled from the live card dashboard, so the layout is judged against the illustrations it will actually carry. These frames are live — tap the rail, the objectives strip, “More”, the filters and the cards. Open any screen full-bleed on a phone with ?screen=home, ?screen=nav, ?screen=more or ?screen=collection.

1 · Home

Full-bleed rotating hero, a collapsed icon rail, and swipeable art sections. The only permanent chrome is the four-atom top row and the five-tab bar.

2 · Expanded quick action

One group open at a time — here Decks has grown from an icon into a gold pill with its three shortcuts floating above the rail. Tapping another collapses it.

3 · “More”, expanded upward

The five permanent tabs stay five. Keep, Social, Profile, Shop, Settings and Help live in a glass sheet that lifts out of the tab bar, and closes any open quick action.

4 · Collection gallery

Browsing as an art gallery: illustrations overflow their plates, search starts as an icon, filters hide behind one control, and a tap lifts the card into a detail sheet.

5 · Card detail

A tap (or long press) lifts the illustration clean out of the grid and onto a sheet that carries only what matters: name, element, rarity and three numbers.
