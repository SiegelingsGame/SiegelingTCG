Siegelings TCG — art-first home & navigation

A minimalist, gallery-led direction for the mobile hub: roughly 70% artwork, 20% navigation, 10% text. Two kinds of real art carry it — cinematic gallery plates across the upper screens, and production overlay cutouts (straight from the live card dashboard) for the cards themselves — so the layout is judged against the illustrations it will actually ship with. These frames are live: tap the rail, the objectives strip, “More”, the filters and the cards. Open any screen full-bleed on a phone with ?screen= plus home, play, collection, decks, shop, profile, nav, more, play-guest or more-guest.

Feature coverage — nothing dropped, only re-homed

The navigation is simpler, not smaller. Every route, dashboard action and panel the shipping hub exposes today is listed below with the place it lives in the new information architecture, and the screen that owns it. Everything is reachable in at most two taps from any screen, because the quick-action rail and the tab bar are on every one of them.

1 · Home

The top of the screen is now a full-bleed cinematic gallery plate that rotates every 7s, with a From the Gallery strip further down. Only the four-atom top row and the two-tier bottom navigation are permanent.

2 · Play

Battle and Siege as full-bleed plates instead of 60 words of body copy — the same two modes the shipping picker offers, with the loadout and lobbies below.

3 · Play, signed out

A guest gets a Sign In in the top HUD where the crest would be, and the account prompt becomes one artwork band rather than a card of copy.

4 · Cards

Browsing as an art gallery: illustrations overflow frameless plates, search starts as an icon, element filters hide behind one control, and a tap lifts the card onto a detail sheet.

5 · Decks

Each deck is a wide artwork panel — its Land behind, its lead Siegeling breaking the right edge — with element icons, record and a win-rate bar. Builder and Auto Build sit on top.

6 · Shop

One cinematic featured offer carrying the whole upper screen, then pack faces using the real elemental card backs, and Siegelcoin bundles as quiet rows rather than loud tiles.

7 · Profile

A gallery plate behind the crest, level ring and XP, three stat tiles, a showcase carousel of the player's own Siegelings, badges, and recent results.

8 · Collection tray

One bar, not two. Collection slides its tray up out of the tab bar — Cards, Decks and Deck Builder now live here rather than in a second header.

9 · Play tray

Keep now sits under Play alongside Arena, Ranked and Siege. Only one tray is ever open, so the bar never becomes a second header again.

10 · More tray, signed out

The same tray leads with Sign In and Create Account for a guest, so the account route exists in the bar as well as on Play.

11 · Card detail

A tap (or long press) lifts the illustration clean out of the grid and onto a sheet that carries only what matters: name, element, rarity and three numbers.
