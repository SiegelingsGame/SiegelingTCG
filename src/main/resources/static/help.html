Basics

Siegelings is a tactical elemental card game. Each player builds a 3×3 board half. Creature cards — Sieglings — fight with abilities paid for by elemental energy. There is no printed ATK/DEF: damage comes from abilities.

  • Player HP starts at 50. When your Siegeling is defeated, you take rarity-based bounty damage.
  • Siegling stats are Health, Speed, notches (directional colored dots), and abilities.
  • Board cap: up to 5 Sieglings per side. Evolutions do not count against the cap or the one-placement-per-turn limit.
  • Win by reducing the opponent’s HP to 0.

Turn loop

Each round cycles through three phases for the active player:

  1. Draw Draw cards into your hand to prepare the turn.
  2. Setup Place at most one new Siegeling, cast spells and traps, and optionally claim surviving Sieglings for temporary energy. Elemental afflictions like Burn and Wither often resolve at the start of your Setup.
  3. Battle All ready Sieglings act in Speed order. Spend energy on abilities; then the next round begins.

Card types

Siegling

Creatures you place on the board. They carry notches, Health, Speed, and battle abilities. Max 3 copies of a card in a deck; full evolution lines only.

Spell

One-shot effects cast from hand during Setup (or when their timing allows). Paid with elemental energy from your links and sockets.

Trap

Reactive cards set for later. They fire when their condition is met — often during the opponent’s actions.

Evolution

Placed onto a live precursor Siegeling on your board. Evolutions skip the placement cap and the one-placement-per-turn limit. Curse blocks evolving that unit.

SiegeKnight

Your trainer. Grants a passive plus an active or ultimate ability that shapes the whole match.

Standard premade decks are 40 cards: 20 Sieglings, 10 spells, 10 traps.

Board, notches & energy

Colored dots on a Siegeling’s edges are notches. When two adjacent cards’ notches point at each other — a reciprocal link — they connect and generate elemental energy.

  • Sockets on the board perimeter can also anchor edge cells (left column LEFT, right column RIGHT, outer row TOP/BOTTOM).
  • Energy pays for spells, traps, and battle abilities. Matching the ability’s element costs matters.
  • Claim (Setup): spend a surviving Siegeling’s presence for temporary energy. Units with Curse cannot be claimed.
  • Placement must stay legal on your half — foundation and network rules decide which cells light up as valid.

Buffs & temporary statuses

Ability effects and trainer passives can apply short-lived badges separate from elemental afflictions:

Shield

Temporary hit points that absorb damage before Health. Cleared when depleted or when temporary effects lapse after Battle.

Damage Boost

Outgoing ability damage is temporarily increased (from abilities, auras, or trainer passives).

Speed Boost

Higher effective Speed — the unit tends to act earlier in the Battle queue.

Max Health

Maximum Health raised (often with a matching heal). Distinct from a Shield stack.

Frozen

Cannot act during Battle. Chill affliction reaches Freeze at 3 stacks until the owner’s next Setup.

Speed Zero

Acts last / effectively locked out of normal Speed ordering.

Weak

This unit is on the wrong side of the elemental matchup against an attacker — expect bonus damage.

Strong

Advantage badge when your element hits a weak defender for +1 damage.

Elemental afflictions

When an elemental attack deals Health damage, it can add a stack of that element’s affliction. Badges show on the board card. Neutral damage does not inflict.

Stacks have caps. Some clear on a tick; others persist until cleansed or consumed by their own rule.

Siege mode

Siege / Adventure is a single-player expedition. Pick a SiegeKnight and starter warband, walk a branching map (battles, elites, rest camps, shops, events, boss), and fight AP-based card battles — drag a card from your hand onto the battlefield, then End Turn.

  • Rules run on the server; the client plays back presentation events.
  • Many battle afflictions and card effects have Siege counterparts (Burn-like DoTs, shields that last until your next turn, party-wide linked effects, and so on).
  • Earn gold, cards, and items as you climb toward the Siegelord.
