Basics

Siegelings is a tactical elemental card game. Each player builds a 3×3 board half. Creature cards — Sieglings — fight with abilities paid for by elemental energy. There is no printed ATK/DEF: damage comes from abilities.

  • Player HP starts at 50. When your Siegeling is defeated, you take rarity-based bounty damage.
  • Siegling stats are Health, Speed, notches (directional colored dots), and abilities.
  • Board cap: up to 5 Sieglings per side. Evolutions do not count against the cap or the one-placement-per-turn limit.
  • Win by reducing the opponent’s HP to 0.

Turn loop

Each round cycles through three phases for the active player:

  1. Draw Draw cards into your hand to prepare the turn.
  2. Setup Place at most one new Siegeling, play Strategies and Deceptions, and optionally claim surviving Siegelings for temporary energy. Elemental afflictions like Burn and Wither often resolve at the start of your Setup.
  3. Battle All ready Sieglings act in Speed order. Spend energy on abilities; then the next round begins.

Card types

Siegling

Creatures you place on the board. They carry notches, Health, Speed, and battle abilities. Max 3 copies of a card in a deck; full evolution lines only.

Strategy

Immediate effects played from hand during Setup (or when their timing allows). Older card data may call this role Spell.

Deception

Reactive cards set face-down for later. They reveal when their condition is met—often during the opponent’s actions. Older data may call this role Trap.

Evolution

Placed onto a live precursor Siegeling on your board. Evolutions skip the placement cap and the one-placement-per-turn limit. Curse blocks evolving that unit.

SiegeKnight

Your trainer. Grants a passive plus an active or ultimate ability that shapes the whole match.

Standard premade decks are 40 cards: 20 Siegelings, 10 Strategies, and 10 Deceptions. Custom deckbuilding unlocks at 30 owned card copies.

Board, notches & energy

Colored dots on a Siegeling’s edges are notches. When two adjacent cards’ notches point at each other — a reciprocal link — they connect and generate elemental energy.

  • Sockets on the board perimeter can also anchor edge cells (left column LEFT, right column RIGHT, outer row TOP/BOTTOM).
  • Energy pays for Strategies, Deceptions, and battle abilities. Matching the card or ability cost matters.
  • Claim (Setup): spend a surviving Siegeling’s presence for temporary energy. Units with Curse cannot be claimed.
  • Placement must stay legal on your half — foundation and network rules decide which cells light up as valid.

Energy pools: Fire, Ice, Earth, Wind, Water, Electric, Metal, Shadow, Psychic, and Undead have dedicated pools. Poison and Light attacks still apply their afflictions, but their action cards use Neutral costs.

Buffs & temporary statuses

Ability effects and trainer passives can apply short-lived badges separate from elemental afflictions:

Shield

Temporary hit points that absorb damage before Health. Cleared when depleted or when temporary effects lapse after Battle.

Damage Boost

Outgoing ability damage is temporarily increased (from abilities, auras, or trainer passives).

Speed Boost

Higher effective Speed — the unit tends to act earlier in the Battle queue.

Max Health

Maximum Health raised (often with a matching heal). Distinct from a Shield stack.

Frozen

Cannot act during Battle. Chill affliction reaches Freeze at 3 stacks until the owner’s next Setup.

Speed Zero

Acts last / effectively locked out of normal Speed ordering.

Weak

This unit is on the wrong side of the elemental matchup against an attacker — expect bonus damage.

Strong

The defender’s element beats the attacker’s, so the hit is resisted for -1 damage (a 1-damage hit is reduced to nothing).

Elemental afflictions

When an elemental attack deals Health damage, it can add a stack of that element’s affliction. Badges show on the board card. Neutral damage does not inflict.

Stacks have caps. Some clear on a tick; others persist until cleansed or consumed by their own rule.

Siege mode

Live · Solo expedition Levels matter here

SiegeKnight XP upgrades passive and active abilities in Siege. Those bonuses never change Standard Battle values.

Siege / Adventure is a run-based expedition. Pick a SiegeKnight and starter warband, cross a branching map of battles, elites, rest camps, shops, events, and a boss, then fight AP-based card battles.

Build a runEarn gold, recruit cards, collect items, and improve the warband as you climb.
Spend APPlay cards from a fresh hand, resolve their actions, and end the turn when your plan is set.
Read Siege rulesThere is no elemental weakness chart in Siege. Its server-driven rules include shields and affliction counterparts, including Leech.

The Gauntlet

Live · Roguelike on the Battle Table Draft ten, climb the floors

Every fight is a real solo Battle Table match. You start each fight at 25 HP. Three losses end the run.

The Gauntlet is a solo climb that uses Battle Table rules (not Siege’s AP battles). Pick an unlocked preset deck and a SiegeKnight, draft 10 balanced cards from that deck (one redraw), then fight floor after floor.

Floors & actsEach act has ten floors. Floor 5 is an elite; floor 10 is a SiegeKnight on 30 HP (plus 5 per act) with a cut-down preset deck. Earlier floors roll wild packs that get tougher as you climb.
RewardsWins pay gold, SiegeKnight XP, and a choice of 3 cards (or 10 gold). Elites and knight floors also grant free forges. Gauntlet fights do not write match history or pay Siegecoins.
Camp toolsBetween floors: forge upgrades onto cards (up to level 6), shop for cards / a life / card removal, and spend knight level-ups on perks (Vitality, Command, Tactics, Plunder, Forgemaster).

A lost floor can be rematched. Quitting or finishing a fight returns you to the Gauntlet camp via Return to the Gauntlet on the Battle Table end screen.

Keep

Live · Sanctuary Restore your stronghold

Assign Siegelings as residents, raise buildings, craft, and hold Akhar’s Wall against raiders.

My Keep is your base outside of card matches. Raise the Woodlot and Storehouse, expand workshops, display decorations, and post defenders on the Wall for Crowns.

Build & craftSpend Timber and materials on Hall upgrades, tool rooms, and decorations. Higher Hall ranks unlock deeper ladders.
ResidentsPost Siegelings to rooms that match their affinities. Helpers speed workshops; resting companions gather in the Enclave.
Akhar’s WallManned posts earn Crowns. Raiders you watch fall for a bounty. Leave the Wall empty and the Keep can take damage.

Chronicles

Live · Idle RPG Train the SiegeKnight

Professions, elemental mastery, Siegeling bonds, and timed expeditions — paid in Crowns, not Siegecoins.

Siegeknight Chronicles is an idle progression layer around your SiegeKnight and company. Train work skills, send the company on expeditions, raise bonds, and spend Crowns at the guild market.

Knight & companyLevel the knight, assign companions, and unlock bond techniques as rapport grows.
Work & expeditionsPark the company on professions or send them on a timed route. Return for XP, materials, and bond gains.
Realm & wildsJoin or found a guild for weekly operations, shop the market, and chase wild encounters when they appear.

Dominion

Test · Keep + Chronicles One combined sandbox

Dominion merges Keep construction with Chronicles progression in a single browser-driven playtest. Progress here is separate from the live Keep and Chronicles saves.

Dominion is the hub’s temporary test band for the combined Keep + Chronicles fantasy: raise Timber production, expand the Hall and workshops, drill Wall defenders, rush builds with Crowns, and walk the knight’s road without leaving the same shell.

Keep systemsWoodlot, Storehouse, Builder’s Yard, workshops, decorations, tool tiers, and Akhar’s Wall with posted defenders and siege waves.
Chronicles systemsKnight skills, companions, Crowns, and expedition-style sinks live in the same UI rather than as a separate page.
Expect changeRules, economy, and UI shift while this mode is under test. Prefer live Keep / Chronicles when you want account progression that sticks.
