Guided Tutorial Match

Learn Arena in a guided match against the Training Dummy. Play as Squire Bob with the Ashen Roots deck and take the first turn. Each step explains a rule and shows you what to do next.

  • The three phases: Draw, Setup and Battle
  • Sockets, notch links, and combo energy
  • Strategies and Deception energy requirements
  • Damage, Siege Damage, status badges, evolutions, and claiming
  • Advanced Tutorial: effects, shields and battle controls
The tutorial provides your deck and SiegeKnight. Replay it as often as you like. Your first tutorial win earns a second starter pack and 250 Siegecoins.
